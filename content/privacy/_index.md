Here is how we handle information when you use [akanreports.com](https://akanreports.com).

## When you visit

Like most websites, the systems that host and deliver our pages receive basic technical details needed to serve the site: things like your IP address, browser type, and which pages you request.

We also use audience measurement to understand how the site is used. That may involve cookies or similar tools, and it may collect usage information such as pages viewed and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

## When you email us

If you write to us, we receive whatever you put in the message, including your email address.

## What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. You do not need an account to read the site, and we do not currently run advertising.

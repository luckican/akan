Ghana's economy expanded in the first quarter, with information and communication technology providing the strongest lift. Mining and services also contributed to the headline figure.

## Growth is not the same as relief

National output can improve before households feel the difference. Wages, food prices, borrowing costs, and where new jobs appear determine whether growth changes daily life.

## What to watch

The next test is whether stronger sectors create employment and public revenue beyond the companies leading the expansion.

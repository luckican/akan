Analysis / Economy

The economy is growing. The recovery is still uneven.

Technology and mining pushed the national number higher, while the pressures households feel every day moved more slowly.

By Kofi Mensah31 Aug 2026Updated 09:15 GMT7 min read

Economic growth accelerated during the first quarter, but the headline figure does not describe a uniform recovery. The strongest gains were concentrated in a small number of sectors, while household purchasing power remained under pressure.

That distinction matters. A national average can move in the right direction even when the benefits arrive slowly—or do not arrive at all—for many people.

Sources and method

This report uses the Ghana Statistical Service quarterly release and sector tables. Figures were last checked on 31 August 2026.

Correction

An earlier version misstated the comparison period. The figure is year-on-year, not quarter-on-quarter.
